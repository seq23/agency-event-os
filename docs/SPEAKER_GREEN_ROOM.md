# Speaker Green Room

## Purpose

The Speaker Green Room is the speaker-facing backstage prep area. It helps speakers understand when they are live, where to join, what version of their script/deck is approved, and what the producer needs from them.

## Core Jobs

- Show speaker call time
- Show countdown to live segment
- Show backstage/rehearsal links
- Show tech-check state
- Show asset checklist
- Show approved script/deck versions
- Show pending last-minute changes
- Show producer messages
- Prevent speakers from assuming unapproved changes are live

## Visibility Rule

Speakers see their own event context. They do not see internal agency notes, other speakers' problems, client drama, contractor/vendor issues, or private production risks.

## Future Persistence

This will eventually read from profiles, speaker_profiles, speaker_assets, speaker_scripts, run_of_show_segments, video_rooms, and last_minute_change_requests.
