# Sponsor Approval Workflow

## Flow

1. Agency creates sponsor package.
2. Sponsor receives setup link.
3. Sponsor submits deliverables.
4. Agency reviews technical/production fit.
5. Client reviews brand/content fit where required.
6. Agency locks final booth/session/mentions.
7. Locked sponsor items feed expo, run of show, ready room, and report.

## Statuses

package_draft, package_sent, package_accepted, assets_requested, assets_submitted, agency_review, client_review, changes_requested, approved, locked, live, fulfilled, reported.
