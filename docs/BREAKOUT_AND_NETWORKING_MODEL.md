# Breakout and Networking Model

## Breakouts

Supports scheduled and ad hoc rooms, workshops, roundtables, sponsor sessions, speaker rehearsals, and green rooms.

## Producer Controls

- open room
- close room
- extend room
- move attendees
- pin speaker
- mute/remove participant
- promote attendee
- start/stop recording

## 3-Minute Networking

Attendees enter a queue, match into a 3-minute video room, connect/skip/report, and rotate to the next match.

## Networking Settings

duration_seconds, matching_mode, queue_open, max_matches_per_attendee, cooldown_seconds, report_enabled.

## Deferred

Real video rooms and provider tokens wait for video provider integration.
