# Asset Approval Matrix

## Purpose

All submitted materials should follow a shared approval model, regardless of whether they come from a speaker, sponsor, client, email, cloud link, or producer upload.

## Asset Types

speaker_headshot, speaker_bio, speaker_deck, speaker_script, speaker_release, speaker_intro, sponsor_logo, sponsor_booth_copy, sponsor_offer, sponsor_video, sponsor_pdf, sponsor_cta, sponsor_rep_info, client_brand_asset, event_slide, replay_asset.

## Ownership

Agency approves technical fit.
Client approves brand/content fit.
Speaker approves their own public bio/headshot/script.
Sponsor approves their booth/offer.
Producer locks final live use.
